Welcome to Minha Qur’an Academy

A supportive online home for Quranic learning

Founded in 2018, Minha Qur’an Academy has been dedicated to teaching children, teenagers, adults, and aspiring Quran teachers worldwide through structured and compassionate online instruction.

For over 8 years, our qualified teachers have helped young learners and families build strong recitation habits, accurate Tajweed pronunciation, and meaningful Islamic character in a safe, caring online environment.
