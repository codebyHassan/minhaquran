Who We Are

Structured, engaging & supportive Quran education

Founded in 2018, Minha Qur’an Academy is an online Quran and Islamic education academy that has been providing structured, engaging learning for children, teenagers, adults, and aspiring Quran teachers worldwide for over 8 years.

Since our inception in 2018, we have focused on correct Quran reading, authentic Tajweed pronunciation, foundational Islamic character building, and practical teacher training in a supportive online environment.
