Teaching Children Online Since 2018

Making online Quran learning fun, interactive & engaging

Teaching children and young learners since 2018, we understand one of the biggest concerns parents have:

My child does not want to sit for an online Quran class.

Children can easily become bored or distracted during online lessons, especially when teaching is repetitive. Since 2018, Minha Qur’an Academy has been using age-appropriate, interactive teaching methods—gamified slides, visual stories, gentle encouragement, and patient certified teachers—to make Quran learning truly enjoyable and memorable for children.

For Parents

Interactive lessons, not lectures

We understand that keeping children focused during an online class can be challenging.

That is why our children’s Quran lessons are designed to be interactive rather than purely lecture-based. Through games, stories, activities, visuals, questions, practice and positive encouragement, we aim to keep children actively involved.

We don’t just teach children Quran online. We create an environment where children can enjoy learning Quran.
