Who We Are

Structured, engaging & supportive Quran education

Minha Qur’an Academy is an online Quran and Islamic education academy providing structured and engaging learning for children, teenagers, adults, and aspiring Quran teachers worldwide.

We focus on correct Quran reading, Tajweed, Islamic education, and practical teacher training in a supportive online environment.
