Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear, direct answers about our courses, schedule flexibility, teaching methods, and enrollment process.

Who can join Minha Qur’an Academy?

Children, teenagers, adults, beginners, advanced learners, and aspiring Quran teachers can join our online programs. We offer tailored tracks for every age group and experience level.

Do you offer Quran classes for children?

Yes. We use age-appropriate and interactive teaching methods—including educational games, stories, visuals, and positive encouragement—to help children learn Quran in an enjoyable and engaging way.

Do you teach Quran with Tajweed?

Yes. We offer both a foundational Quran with Tajweed course (covering essential rules, Makharij, and Sifaat) and an Advanced Tajweed course for students looking to refine their recitation further.

What is the Noorani / Madani Qaida Course?

It is a step-by-step foundation course designed to help beginners learn Arabic letters, sounds, Harakat (vowels), joining letters, and the basic skills required for accurate Quran reading.

Do you offer Islamic Studies?

Yes. Islamic Studies are offered through structured online lessons according to the student’s age and learning level, helping build a strong moral and spiritual foundation.

What is the curriculum and structure of the Quran Teacher Training course?

The program runs for approximately 2–4 months using a flexible hybrid learning model: structured pre-recorded classes combined with live interactive Zoom sessions and daily one-to-one mentorship. The curriculum comprises 10 comprehensive practical modules:

  1. Complete Noor Al-Bayan Learning Program
  2. Comprehensive English Tajweed Training
  3. Arabic Language Learning for Non-Arabic Speakers
  4. Islamic Education in English
  5. English Communication and Speaking Skills
  6. Designing Interactive Educational Games
  7. Classroom Management and Teaching Techniques
  8. Practical Canva Training Workshop
  9. Hands-On Teaching Practice & Live Demos
  10. Online Academy Development and Digital Marketing
Will I receive a certificate and job opportunities after completing the course?

Yes. Upon successful completion of all 10 modules and practical teaching evaluations, you will be awarded an official Certificate of Completion. Furthermore, Minha Qur’an Academy provides dedicated career development support, offering potential employment opportunities for skilled graduates within our own academy and across our network of partner international online academies.

How are the classes conducted?

Classes are conducted online using suitable digital platforms and teaching resources (such as Zoom or Google Meet), allowing students to learn comfortably from home with screen sharing, digital whiteboards, and interactive tools.

How can I enroll?

You can fill out our online Enrollment Form or reach out directly via WhatsApp or email on our Contact Page. We will get back to you promptly to discuss your learning goals.
