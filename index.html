Welcome to Minha Qur’an Academy

A supportive online home for Quranic learning

Minha Qur’an Academy is an online Quran and Islamic education academy providing structured and engaging learning for children, teenagers, adults, and aspiring Quran teachers worldwide.

We focus on correct Quran reading, Tajweed, Islamic education, and practical teacher training in a supportive online environment.

More about our academy
Our Mission

Making Quranic and Islamic education accessible, engaging and easy to learn.

Through structured online teaching, we also aim to train aspiring Quran teachers with practical teaching and online teaching skills.

  • Read the Quran correctly

  • Build confidence in Quran learning

  • Develop a strong foundation in Islamic education
